Synthetic IR Cluster Explorer

400 fully-synthetic entities and their 1,747 surface variants, one card per entity.

The identity-resolution stress set, generated by ir/generate.py at seed 1. It produces 400 synthetic entities — 246 Identity (people), 154 Institution (orgs) — each a cluster of surface variants, for 1,747 surfaces total at a mean of 4.37 per entity (median 4, max 12). Only the Identity, Street, and Institution families go to IR. Every value comes from inline synthetic wordlists, so the data is PII-free, reproducible from the seed, and safe to share.

The variation is grounded in a real CRM anonymization-mapping distribution — roughly three surfaces per entity, about 65% case-variants, 14% typos, 13% abbreviations, and 7% legal suffixes, with a tail of @handles, #hashtags, domain forms, and Name-by-Other composites — but the generator reads none of that data. It reproduces the failure modes the redesign targets: fuzzy surface variation and shared-first-name collisions, the '100 Janes' problem.

The lexical-only baseline scores low here by design — pairwise P/R/F 0.16/0.10/0.12, B3 0.70/0.33/0.44 — because without embeddings, fuzzy variants never become candidates and shared names collide. That is the motivation for the redesign's reused span embeddings and Enterprise Activity Graph (EAG) co-occurrence signal. Search, filter by family, and sort by size; each card shows an entity's surface values and their types.
