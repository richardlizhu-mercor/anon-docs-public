Snapping over-extends regression

When a model already emits the exact span (mostly qwen, which generates literal surfaces), snapping can push the boundary into attached punctuation or a neighboring token, turning an exact match into a miss. This is why snapping should be applied per-model (encoders yes, qwen no).

Snapping can't fix it out of reach

Boundary repair only helps when the drift is sub-word. Where the GT span has a genuinely different extent — a coarse multi-field GT span (e.g. Name <email> as one span) or the model flagged a different region — a whitespace snap cannot reconstruct it.
