Findings

  • Domain-dependent even on these 3 types: DeBERTa wins clean pii-bench-v4 (~0.93 vs 0.77); Gemini wins real contigo v3.3 (0.94 vs ~0.85).
  • Email is near-solved by all (~0.86–0.99). Street is hardest — Gemini splits addresses; contigo v3.1/v3.2 GT typing barely labels street.
  • Recall vs precision: Gemini higher recall, DeBERTa higher precision — the split that drives the contigo v3.1/v3.2 flip.
