Contigo-bench: PII cluster types — prevalence & detection F1

Every GT span (v3.1 diff + v3.2 mapping) and every deberta-v3-base-pb2-fixdrop prediction was typed into the taxonomy's cluster types — identity, street, institution, other.

How spans were typed. Pattern-obvious surfaces mapped deterministically (email→identity, URL/IP/ID→other, v3.2 mapping-source hints); the 14,076 semantic-ambiguous surfaces (name vs org vs product vs street vs word) were classified agentically (47 parallel agents) per the taxonomy definitions — 23,215 unique surfaces typed in total. Taxonomy: identity = person name / nickname / username / email; street = street-address line only; institution = brand / product / organization (incl. legal entity, DBA, ticker).

Overall prevalence (full corpus)

Identity dominates. Street is rare in this influencer/CRM/messaging data; institution ~7–8%. v3.1 (diff) carries far more other (markup/URL/ID scaffolding it over-labels); v3.2 (mapping) is nearly all real PII.
