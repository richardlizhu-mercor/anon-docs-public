How to read this. Exact = predicted boundaries equal the GT span's exactly (char offsets). det-recall = share of a type's GT spans qwen drew the exact boundary for, regardless of the label it gave. fine P / R / F1 = the stricter test: exact boundary and correct type. mislabel gap = det-recall − fine-recall, i.e. the share it located but typed wrong. What the numbers say

  • Errors are boundary, not type. The mislabel gap is ≤0.02 for every type (worst: Full Name at 0.020) — when qwen misses on exact-span it almost always drew the wrong boundary, not the wrong label. det-recall ≈ fine-recall throughout.
  • Format-shaped types are strong: URL/Domain/Subdomain, Phone Number, Date of Birth, GPS Coordinates all clear 0.90 exact F1 — their boundaries are unambiguous (a URL, a phone, coordinates).
  • Free-text boundary types are the weak spots: Username, Street Address, Email Address sit lowest (Username 0.556) — where the span edge is a judgment call, exact-match punishes drift.
  • The big-support types set the overall: Full Name, ID and Organization Name are ~62% of GT spans and land 0.79 / 0.85 / 0.78, anchoring the 0.788 overall.
