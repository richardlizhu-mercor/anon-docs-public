TL;DR

The pipeline runs end-to-end and the anonymization is real and internally consistent — the same person is faked identically across name variants and their email, and each domain maps to one fake domain everywhere. Two quality quirks stand out: date-like fields fall through to a nonsense char-scramble, and a lowercase organization name bucket over-detects non-PII (fonts, package IDs).

3 · The identity-resolution payoff

One person, one identity — everywhere

4 · Quality quirks worth a human eye

Dates fall through to a char-scramble

Strings typed Date of Birth that are actually date ranges / weekdays hit the format-preserving fallback and come out as nonsense:
BeforeAfter
01 Apr 2026 - 31 Mar 2027→99 Qtq 3852 - 07 Hrh 3173
Fri, 13 Feb 2026→Haj, 35 Zkv 7213

Lowercase organization name over-detects non-PII

The 341-entry lowercase bucket swept in things that aren't PII — font names and package IDs — and faked them anyway:
BeforeAfter
Uber Move Text→Perimode Move Text
com.bajaj.creatr→com.helios.dropmorning

Harmless to privacy, but noise — exactly the kind of thing the detected-entities human-eval work is meant to catch.
