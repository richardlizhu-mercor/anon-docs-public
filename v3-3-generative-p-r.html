contigo v3.3 — Generative (LLM-adjudicated) P/R

The v3.3 GT is imprecise on boundaries (clips Nikhil M→Nikhil). This estimates P/R by having 25 LLM judges score every label the 4 detectors produced on a 500-doc sample semantically — crediting near-misses, flagging genuine junk.

The GT's recall is fine. Of 437 real PII entities, the strict GT missed only 15 (3.4%). The imprecision is a boundary effect, not missing entities. So the real story is precision: Gemini has 5 false positives across 500 docs; A/C have 66/62 (the _→Street kind).

What it reveals

  • Gemini is dramatically the most precise (5 FPs, P 0.987) and keeps top recall → best detector (F1 0.921).
  • A and C over-fire (66/62 genuine FPs) — spurious labels on junk tokens. Their true precision (~0.82) is lower than the strict GT flattered it (~0.91–0.97).
  • B is precise (0.934) but lower recall (0.680).
  • Near-misses (Nikhil M, Asad K) are now credited — but judging semantically mostly exposes A/C's real over-firing that the strict GT masked.

Method

500 random v3.3 cells (460 w/ PII + 40 negatives) · 25 LLM judges, ~20 cells each · each true PII marked with which models caught it + each model's genuine FPs · P = caught/(caught+FP), R = caught/total-PII.
