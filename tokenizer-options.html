Which is best

  • Terabytes → deberta_ft: 0.874 accuracy, $167/TB, a full TB in ~7 hrs on 8×H100. Best accuracy-per-dollar by a wide margin.
  • Want the extra accuracy → routed: 0.910 at $498/TB — pay 3× compute for +0.036 (still ~485× cheaper than Gemini).
  • Gemini is the accuracy leader (0.919) but out at TB scale: ~$242k–482k/TB and ~153 days at the current deploy. Keep it only below ~3M docs/month.
  • Dominated: gliner2_ft, gliner_know_large/base/urchade, piiranha — lower accuracy than deberta_ft at equal-or-higher cost. Excluded: qwen-7B (23× compute), openai_pf (slow+mid), spaCy (not PII-complete). presidio / gliner_know_large / piiranha are useful as cheap pre-filters or weak-label sources.

Why local wins on runtime too

Local models are embarrassingly parallel — linear with GPUs: deberta_ft does 1 TB in ~2.3 days on 1 H100, ~7 hrs on 8, ~33 min on 100 — add GPUs, go faster, no ceiling but budget. Gemini does not scale with your hardware — it's capped by Vertex quota + worker count (~100 docs/s at the current 2 replicas → 153 days/TB; the quota ceiling needs a ~250-worker fan-out, then it's RPM/TPM-bound). Rate limits are not the constraint (0 model-serving 429s in 14 days of prod) — cost and throughput scaling are.

Caveats

  • Local runtime/$ are compute-ratio estimates (MFU 35%), not measured on a real GPU — ±2–3×; the frontier ordering + the ~1,000× Gemini gap survive that. A 1-hour measured deberta_ft run on a real L4/A100 would firm up the numbers.
  • Accuracy is anobench locked-test char-F1; Gemini's 0.919 is partly home-field, and the fine-tuned locals used distilled labels only — real gold would lift them.
  • 1 TB = tokenizable text (~1.32B docs @ 756 chars); raw-file TB extract to less. $/TB is compute only (excludes one-time fine-tune + fleet ops).
