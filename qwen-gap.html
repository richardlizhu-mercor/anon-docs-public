Attribution — what the gap actually is

Reading the categories together, the 0.186-vs-0.794 gap decomposes as:

1. GT coarseness & markup contamination (the biggest slice). 75% of Contigo FPs are qwen spans sitting inside a broader GT span, and 26% of FNs are the mirror. 48% of all Contigo GT spans contain markup (< > = " { }) vs 10% on pii-bench. The self-referential diff captures whole changed regions — HTML/CSS wrappers, brackets, adjacent fields — so qwen's clean entity can never match exactly.

2. GT flags non-PII that merely changed. 71% of Contigo FN is “missed,” but 51% of those are markup / non-PII (font lists, XML namespaces) that qwen correctly ignores. So about half the recall gap is GT noise, not a real miss.

3. GT incompleteness. 19% of Contigo FPs overlap no GT — many are real entities (companies, mail hosts) the pipeline never masked. qwen is right; the GT is short.

4. The genuine part. What remains — the non-markup half of “missed” — is a true out-of-distribution recall gap: brand names (Kofluence) and formats qwen wasn't trained on. pii-bench is in-distribution and hand-curated, so none of 1–3 apply and qwen scores 0.794.

Bottom line: roughly two-thirds of the exact-match gap is a Contigo GT-format artifact (qwen penalized for being cleaner/more complete than a diff-derived GT); about one-third is a genuine OOD recall gap. The exact-match number understates qwen on Contigo.
