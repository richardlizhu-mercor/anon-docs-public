Word-Snapped Exact-Match Re-scoring

The detectors emit incomplete / boundary-drifted spans. Here each predicted span is expanded left and right to the nearest whitespace (snapped to whole whitespace-delimited tokens) before scoring exact-span match against the ground truth. This is a cheap decode post-process, applied to the same predictions — no re-inference. New doc; the raw exact-match results are untouched.

The snap. A predicted span's start moves left while the previous char is non-space, and its end moves right while the next char is non-space:
GT Vanshita,   model → Vanshita  →  snap → Vanshita,  ✓ now exact
This directly targets the token-classifier artifact where the leading subword of a word is dropped (Vanshita→shita). exact raw = no snap · SNAP-pred = snap predictions only, match vs GT as-is (the requested metric) · snap both = snap GT too (word-boundary ceiling).

Takeaway. A whitespace snap is a near-free decode fix that recovers most of the encoders' exact-span loss (the leading-subword-drop artifact), so it's worth adding to the DeBERTa/PF decode path. It marginally over-extends an already-exact generator like qwen, so apply it per-model (encoders yes, qwen no) or compare on the snap both word-boundary basis.
