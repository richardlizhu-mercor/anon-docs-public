Across all four the shape holds: deberta is the more precise detector, Gemini the higher-recall one. On pii-bench-v2 (in-distribution) deberta's precision wins F1 at every lens (char 0.759 vs 0.680), converging by exact span (0.542 vs 0.523). On Contigo (OOD) Gemini's recall advantage flips it — Gemini wins at every lens (char 0.617 vs 0.439). Exact-span collapses on Contigo (~0.19–0.22 both): boundary drift on messy SaaS text.
