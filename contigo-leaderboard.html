Anonymization Detection Benchmark

PII detectors ranked by how closely they reproduce what our anonymization pipeline actually masked on real customer data (kofluence / Contigo). The benchmark is built by diffing the raw extraction against the anonymized output (the ground-truth artifacts our coworker published to Google Drive): every word-run the pipeline changed becomes a ground-truth PII span, in the raw document's character offsets. Scoring is char-level, type-agnostic detection (a character is a hit if it falls in any predicted span and any GT span) — micro-averaged P / R / F1, overall and per service.

Ground truth is self-referential. It measures agreement with the current pipeline, not absolute correctness — a model can only score 1.0 by masking exactly what the pipeline masked. High precision + low recall therefore means “more conservative than the pipeline,” not necessarily “wrong.” Eval set: a 15,223-doc balanced sample (cap 3,000/service, ~25% held as no-PII for false-positive signal) drawn from a 1,680,579-doc / 7-service corpus (2.79M spans). Full corpus + eval set live at s3://data-acq-anonymized-prod/contigo/benchmark/. All models run on GKE L4 (encoders, $0.71/hr) or Modal (6 models total).

What this benchmark shows
  • Overall F1 falls as models get bigger — 184M deberta-v3-base tops the board (0.699) and 7.6B qwen is last. This inverts the in-distribution pii-bench ranking: bigger models are more conservative here (precision holds ~0.78–0.82, but recall collapses from ~0.62 to ~0.19 out-of-distribution).
  • Per-service behavior is heterogeneous — architecture matches modality. Every encoder collapses on teams (HTML-wrapped mentions); the generative qwen model uniquely leads on drive (OOXML docs) and zoho; but on tabular mysql the big deberta-v2 LoRA models win outright (xxlarge 0.82). “Best overall” ≠ “best everywhere” — pick the detector per service.
  • Precision is stable, recall is the differentiator. Ranking is essentially a recall ranking — the cheap small encoder recovers the most of what the pipeline masked.
